Project Overview

Developed an AI-powered video analysis model using UNet segmentation to detect key moments in slow-motion airbag deployment videos. The model identified critical deployment moments, such as when the airbag rips through the seat, reaches shoulder level, and fully inflates.

The Outcome

Replaced manual video-based quality inspection with an automated AI solution for automotive safety validation, significantly reducing review time while achieving 97%+ accuracy by integrating SAM (Segment Anything Model) as a second-layer refinement.
