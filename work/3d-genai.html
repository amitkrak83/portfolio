The Problem

Prototyping automotive components like side glasses and steering side handles required manual scripting and slow iterations in CAD software. The design team needed a way to translate conceptual ideas directly into 3D models to speed up early-stage prototyping.

What I Built

Built a Proof of Concept (POC) for a Fortune 500 automotive client. I fine-tuned Large Language Models (LLMs) to understand natural language design instructions and translate them directly into executable CadQuery scripts.

The system bridges the gap between text prompts and complex 3D geometries, allowing the generation of workable 3D CAD files that can be further refined in tools like Blender.

The Outcome

Demonstrated the ability to generate 3D models of car parts directly from text prompts, validating the potential to drastically reduce early-stage prototyping time and empowering designers to iterate at the speed of thought.
